French Bulldog vs. English Bulldog: Which Bulldog Is Right for Your Family? 🐶❤️
So, you decided you need a bulldog?
Absolutely. Everyone needs a bulldog. 😄
Some people just haven’t realized it yet.
The only real question is:
Which bulldog is right for you — a French Bulldog or an English Bulldog?
Now, technically, we could start with size, temperament, exercise needs, and all those responsible grown-up things.
But where’s the fun in that? 😏
First, we need to answer a much more important question:
Are You a French Bulldog Person or an English Bulldog Person? 🐾
Welcome to the Highly Scientific Bulldog Personality Test. 🔬🐶
It has not been approved by any university.
No researchers were involved.
But we live with a lot of bulldogs, which honestly may be more useful here. 😂
Let’s begin.
Question #1: What Does Your Perfect Saturday Look Like? ☕🌤️
A. Coffee, a little drive, some errands, maybe lunch somewhere, and no real plan for what happens next.
B. Coffee on the porch, nowhere I urgently need to be, a nice walk later, and absolutely nobody asking me to hurry.
Picked A?
Keep the French Bulldog in mind. 👀
Picked B?
An English Bulldog has already pulled up a chair beside you. 🪑🐶
Question #2: How Important Is Personal Space? 🚪😅
A. Very important.
Hmm.
We may have a problem.
B. I can compromise.
Better.
C. I am comfortable having a wrinkled creature follow me from room to room, supervise my cooking, listen to my phone calls, and wait outside the bathroom as though I might escape through a secret tunnel.
Excellent. 😂
You are bulldog material.
Question #3: When You Talk to Your Dog, What Kind of Response Do You Expect? 🗣️🐶
A. A polite tail wag.
B. Maybe some eye contact.
C. Facial expressions, head tilts, grunts, dramatic sighs, strange little noises, emotional reactions, and at least one moment every day when I genuinely wonder whether my dog is about to answer me in English.
If you chose C…
there is a very good chance you are a Frenchie person. 😄
French Bulldogs don’t simply listen to conversations.
They participate.
Sometimes quite loudly. 🎤
And their vocal range deserves special recognition.
A Frenchie can sound like a baby 👶, an elderly gentleman complaining about customer service, a tiny dinosaur 🦖, a squeaky door, or a pop star making a very experimental career choice. 🎶
Spend enough time with one and eventually you will say:
“This dog is going to start talking.”
And every Frenchie owner will understand exactly what you mean. 😂
Question #4: What Kind of Companion Are You Looking For? 🐕💞
A. Someone who wants to know where I’m going, what I’m doing, why I’m doing it, and why they weren’t consulted first.
B. Someone who is simply happy to be beside me — warm, solid, loving, and absolutely in no hurry to complicate a perfectly good day.
If you chose A, welcome to life with a French Bulldog.
A Frenchie doesn’t simply live in your home.
He moves into your life.
Completely.
You go to the kitchen?
Frenchie. 🍗
You sit down to work?
Frenchie. 💻
You move six feet to another chair?
Congratulations.
Both of you have relocated. 😂
You may technically be capable of living without your Frenchie for a few hours.
Your Frenchie, however, sees absolutely no reason why he should ever be required to test that theory.
A French Bulldog is part clown 🤡, part child 👶, and unquestionably your best friend ❤️ — with enough charisma for a dog three times his size.
Young Frenchies can absolutely be little tornadoes. 🌪️
But as they grow older, many become much calmer.
The zoomies may slow down.
The comedy does not. 😏
Neither does the curiosity, the empathy, or that incredible ability to become your second skin.
A three-year-old Frenchie doesn’t need to race around the house to make you laugh.
Sometimes one eyebrow is enough. 👀
That is an advanced level of comedy.
Question #5: What Kind of Walk Sounds Perfect? ☕🐕🌿
A. Let’s see what’s around the corner.
B. Nice weather. Nice view. No rush. We’ll get there when we get there.
C. A pleasant walk followed by coffee somewhere beautiful sounds pretty close to perfect.
If you are somewhere between B and C, we need to talk about the English Bulldog. 🐶
An English Bulldog has a wonderful ability to remind you that not everything in life needs to be done at full speed.
A walk can simply be a walk. 🚶‍♀️🐕
You can look around.
Enjoy the weather. 🌤️
Stop for a minute.
Sit outside afterward.
Have coffee. ☕
And beside you is this broad, wrinkled, wonderfully solid creature who seems perfectly satisfied with the fact that you are both exactly where you are supposed to be.
There is an incredible warmth to an English Bulldog. ❤️
They are powerful dogs, but underneath all that muscle is an almost ridiculous amount of tenderness.
They lean against you.
Rest that enormous head on your leg.
Follow you from room to room while pretending that this has absolutely nothing to do with you. 😏
You move to the porch.
A minute later…
bulldog.
Pure coincidence, obviously. 😂
There is something about having an English Bulldog beside you that makes the whole room feel a little calmer.
Question #6: Your English Bulldog Is Lying Peacefully Across the Room. Someone Rolls His Favorite Ball. 🎾
What happens?
Nothing.
Still nothing.
He appears to be asleep. 😴
You begin to wonder whether he even noticed.
And then—
GOOD LORD, WHO FIRED THE BULLDOG? 💥🐶
The slightly clumsy bear cub has disappeared.
You now have a bulldog-shaped projectile. 🚀
All that muscle, which apparently had been on standby for the last hour, has activated at once.
Where was that speed when you called him five minutes ago?
Nobody knows. 🤷‍♀️
English Bulldog physics remains poorly understood.
And then there is The Ball. 🎾
Not a ball.
The Ball.
Some English Bulldogs develop a relationship with one particular toy that probably deserves its own psychological study. 😂
He brings it to you because he wants you to throw it.
You reach for it.
Absolutely not.
It is his ball.
You explain that throwing requires temporary possession of the ball.
He rejects your proposal.
Negotiations begin. 🤝
Eventually, you throw it.
He launches himself after it as though generations of family honor depend on the outcome. 💨🐶
Then he brings it back.
And negotiations begin again.
Question #7: What Kind of Comedy Do You Prefer? 😂🎭
A. Someone who knows exactly how funny they are and keeps performing because you laughed once.
B. Someone who has absolutely no idea they are funny, bumps into a chair, looks offended by the chair, and walks away as though nothing happened.
Option A?
French Bulldog. 🤡🐶
Option B?
English Bulldog. 🐻🐶
Both?
Well…
keep reading. 😏
French Bulldog vs. English Bulldog Personality: The Short Version 🐾
The French Bulldog wants to be part of everything you do.
The English Bulldog wants to be beside you while you do it.
The Frenchie asks:
“What are we doing? I’m coming too!” 🏃‍♂️🐶
The English Bulldog asks:
“Whatever we’re doing, can we do it somewhere comfortable?” 🛋️
The Frenchie comments on your decisions.
The English Bulldog quietly evaluates them. 👀
The Frenchie can make an ordinary Tuesday feel like a full production. 🎬
The English Bulldog can make an ordinary Sunday morning feel like exactly enough. ☕🌤️
One is a comedian, a child, a shadow, and your best friend all rolled into one compact package.
The other is strength and tenderness somehow living in the same wrinkled body — a slightly clumsy bear one moment and a surprisingly powerful force the next.
And both have enough personality to make the words “just a dog” sound completely ridiculous. ❤️
Which Bulldog Is Better for Your Family? 🏡🐶
That may actually be the wrong question.
Neither breed is simply “better.”
The question is:
Which personality feels more like home to you?
Do you want the expressive little shadow who joins every conversation?
French Bulldog. 🐾
Do you want the strong, gentle companion whose presence somehow makes everything feel calmer?
English Bulldog. ❤️
Do you love a dog who communicates with his entire face?
Frenchie. 😄
Do you love the contradiction of a powerful little tank who turns into pure softness the second he leans against you?
English Bulldog. 🐻
Do you want personal space?
We may suggest a goldfish. 🐠😂
So… What Did We Learn? 🤔🐾
After this extremely serious and scientifically questionable test, you should now know whether you are a French Bulldog person or an English Bulldog person.
Probably.
Maybe.
Because there is one small problem.
Life is not always that simple.
Some days, you need a Frenchie.
You need the jokes. 😂
The expressions. 👀
The ridiculous noises. 🎤
The tiny shadow with bat ears who follows you everywhere and somehow turns the most ordinary day into a story.
And some days, you need an English Bulldog.
You need that warm, solid presence beside you.
The slightly clumsy bear who makes you laugh just by walking across the room…
and then settles against your leg and somehow makes the whole world feel quieter. ❤️
One brings the laughter, the commentary, the drama, and the feeling that you will never do anything alone again.
The other brings warmth, tenderness, strength, and the peaceful comfort of having your best friend right beside you.
One keeps life interesting. 🎭
The other reminds you to slow down long enough to enjoy it. ☕
One says:
“What are we doing? I’m coming too!” 🐶💨
The other says:
“Whatever we’re doing, bring the coffee.” ☕🐶
So perhaps the real question isn’t:
French Bulldog or English Bulldog?
Maybe the real question is:
Which One Comes First? 😏❤️
Because the longer you live with bulldogs, the harder it becomes to choose between these two sides of life.
And at some point, you may discover that they don’t compete with each other at all.
They complement each other.
Almost perfectly.
Which means your biggest problem may eventually have nothing to do with choosing a bulldog.
It may simply be figuring out…
where you’re going to put the bigger sofa. 🛋️🐶🐶❤️
